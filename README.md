# About me
Hello! I am an applied economist at Vrije Universiteit Brussel (VUB), where I serve as a postdoctoral researcher and visiting professor in the Faculty of Social Sciences & Solvay Business School. My research examines political polarisation, populism and democratic representation, alongside an emerging agenda on the political economy of AI, technological change and societal transformation. I combine econometric analysis with computational social science methods to study society and its institutions at the intersection of politics and economics. 

In addition to my research activities, I have served as course holder for Research Methods and as a teaching assistant for Microeconomics and Public Choice. I have also supervised numerous students in their bachelor's and master's thesis projects. In these roles, I encourage students to explore the social, political and cultural dimensions of economic phenomena. Student feedback has been particularly positive about being challenged to engage with topics and perspectives that are not typically covered in standard economics and business programmes.
